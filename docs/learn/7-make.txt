These directories contain your persistent data:
- MariaDB
    WordPress settings, appearance configuration, users, posts, comments.
- WordPress
    Themes, plugins, uploads, and WordPress files.

Even though make fclean removes Docker's named volume definitions, 
it does not delete the underlying host directories.


Bind-mounted data means data stored directly on your computer (the host), rather than inside a Docker container.

In your Inception project, you're using bind mounts to make your WordPress and MariaDB data persistent.

Host directory	    Contains
~/data/mariadb	    WordPress users, passwords (hashed), posts, comments, settings, theme configuration
~/data/wordpress	WordPress PHP files, installed themes, plugins, uploaded images


----

Why is this important?
Without persistent storage, deleting a container could delete its data.


With your bind-mounted volumes:
    make down
    make up

Your WordPress data remains.

will preserve your data as long as the host directories remain intact.

---

Container: Runs your application.
Bind mount: Connects a folder on your computer to a folder inside the container.
Persistent data: Survives container deletion and recreation.
